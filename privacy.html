Privacy Policy 私隱政策

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Who uses this app

Hermes Personal Assistant is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses and why

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Drive (to store backups of its configuration), Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Docs and Sheets, solely to perform tasks the owner asks for.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the owner's computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
